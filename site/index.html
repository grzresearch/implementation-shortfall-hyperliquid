Costs are bps of filled notional for a market order of the selected size, relative to the arrival mid: spread + impact + taker fee. “≈” marks a cost that reaches into aggregated buckets beyond the 20 full-precision levels (exact inside them, upper bound beyond); “≥” marks an order larger than the whole visible book (cost of the fillable part). Click a column to sort, a row to inspect.

Method. For each market the live L2 book (20 full-precision levels per side, extended by an aggregated book for the depth beyond them) is walked exactly as code/simulator.py does, and the cost is decomposed as in code/is_decomp.py: spread (crossing the touch), impact (walking the book) and fee (effective taker rate). Opportunity cost needs the post-trade price path and is only available in the paper. Maker rows assume the order is filled and earn the half-spread versus the arrival mid; adverse selection is not priced. Fees follow the published Hyperliquid formula with growth mode, deployer fee scale and quote asset read live per market; the trader profile is yours to set.

Data. api.hyperliquid.xyz info endpoint and WebSocket, queried directly from your browser within the public rate limit. Paper benchmarks come from results/summary_stats.csv (101 markets, 15 Dec 2025 – 12 May 2026). See docs/LIVE_DASHBOARD.md and docs/FEE_CALIBRATION.md. Not investment advice.
